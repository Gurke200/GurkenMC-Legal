GurkenMC Privacy Policy Last updated: August 21, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how GurkenMC handles information when users interact with the GurkenMC Discord Bot and connected services. 1. Information We May Process Depending on the features being used, GurkenMC may process limited information such as Discord user IDs, Discord server IDs, Discord channel IDs, usernames or display names, bot commands and interactions, configuration settings, TikTok account identifiers, TikTok profile information made available through authorized API access, and technical information required to operate and secure the service. GurkenMC does not require users to provide their Discord or TikTok passwords. 2. TikTok Data When a user connects a TikTok account or uses TikTok-related functionality, GurkenMC may access information through TikTok's official APIs. The exact information accessed depends on the permissions authorized by the user and the features provided by the bot. 3. How Information Is Used Information may be used to operate the Discord bot, provide requested commands and features, connect Discord and TikTok functionality, save server or user preferences, prevent abuse and security incidents, diagnose technical problems, and improve the reliability of the service. Personal information is not sold to advertisers. 4. Data Sharing GurkenMC does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide the service, including Discord, TikTok, and hosting or infrastructure providers. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service. 5. Data Storage GurkenMC aims to store only information that is reasonably necessary to operate the bot. Information may be deleted when it is no longer required for the service, security purposes, or legitimate operational needs. 6. Data Security Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. 7. User Choices and Data Deletion Users may stop using GurkenMC at any time. Where applicable, users may request deletion of stored information associated with their Discord or TikTok account. Users may also revoke TikTok permissions through their TikTok account settings. 8. Children's Privacy GurkenMC is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from users who are below the minimum age required to use Discord, TikTok, or the applicable service in their country. 9. Third-Party Platforms GurkenMC relies on third-party services including Discord and TikTok. Those services have their own privacy practices, and GurkenMC is not responsible for how those platforms independently process user information. 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when GurkenMC features, legal requirements, or third-party platform requirements change. 11.
